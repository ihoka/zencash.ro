## Final Recommendations

### This Pitch Deck Needs Two Versions

**Version A: Friends & Family / Angel ($100K)**
- Emphasize owned land advantage
- Conservative financial projections (42% ROI)
- Focus on proof-of-concept validation
- Personal relationship and trust-based
- Revenue share or modest equity (20%)
- Detailed operating plan
- This is what you should build NOW

**Version B: Series A / Institutional ($1-2M)**
- Create AFTER successful proof of concept
- Show 12-18 months of actual mining data
- Demonstrate unit economics at scale
- Path to $1M+ annual revenue
- Acquisition by larger mining operation exit
- Build this in 2-3 years

### Don't Try to Make $100K Look Like $3M

The current deck suffers from trying to be a venture-scale pitch when it's actually a bootstrapped proof-of-concept. That's not bad - it's actually better for investor risk/reward at this stage.

**Embrace the small scale advantages:**
- Faster to deploy
- Easier to manage
- Lower risk
- Proves model before scaling
- Doesn't require VC returns
- Founder can stay hands-on

### The Land Is Your Superpower

I cannot stress enough: **OWNED LAND IN ROMANIA IS YOUR BIGGEST ADVANTAGE** and it's completely missing from the current deck.

This single factor:
- Saves €20-40K (20-40% of budget)
- Enables immediate deployment
- Provides expansion path
- Differentiates from every competitor
- Makes $100K viable when it otherwise wouldn't be

**This should be in:**
- Slide 1 (opening hook)
- Slide 3 (solution)

- Slide 12 (investment thesis)

Current deck: 18-month break-even
Reality: 24-30 month break-even

**The honest timeline is better** because:
- Sets realistic expectations
- Builds trust with conservative approach
- Protects you from "why aren't we profitable yet?" questions
- Allows you to beat guidance rather than miss it
