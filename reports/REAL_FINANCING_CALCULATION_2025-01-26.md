## Revenue Projections

### 8kW Solar System Capacity
- **Peak Production:** 8 kW × 5 sun-hours/day = 40 kWh/day
- **Realistic Production:** 30-35 kWh/day average (accounting for weather)
- **Annual Production:** ~12,000 kWh/year

### Mining Revenue (Conservative)

**With 2 Miners (Bootstrap Option):**
- **Power Consumption:** 2 × 3kW = 6 kW continuous
- **Daily Energy Need:** 6 kW × 24h = 144 kWh/day
- **Solar Coverage:** 30 kWh / 144 kWh = **21% solar powered**
- **Grid Supplement Needed:** 114 kWh/day × €0.10/kWh = €11.40/day
- **Mining Revenue:** 2 × $5.50/day = **$11/day**
- **Net Revenue:** $11 - $11.86 (grid cost) = **~$0/day** ⚠️

**Issue:** Running miners 24/7 with only 8kW solar requires significant grid power, eliminating profit.

**SOLUTION: Solar-Only Operation Strategy**
- **Run miners only during solar production hours (6-8 hours/day)**
- **4 miners during peak sun (4-6 hours):** 4 × $5.50/day × (5h/24h) = $4.58/day
- **2 miners on battery (2-3 hours):** 2 × $5.50/day × (2.5h/24h) = $1.15/day
- **Total Daily Revenue:** $4.58 + $1.15 = **$5.73/day**
- **Monthly Revenue:** $5.73 × 30 = **$172/month** (~€165/month / 830 RON/month)
